Serene Desk brand guide · draft

Lamp light at dusk

Serene Desk sells standing desks and small, calm accessories for people who work from home. The whole brand is one image: a dim room at the end of the day, oak furniture, and one warm lamp that shows you where to look. Nothing shouts. The only bright thing on any page is the thing you should do next.

  • Products are called desks, lamps and mats. Never "zen" anything; the calm comes from the design, not the copy.
  • The AI agent is Wren, named for a small, quiet bird. Short answers, no exclamation marks.

Color

Slate and oak make up the room. Lamp gold is the single accent, saved for calls to action, the mic button and focus states. Ember is only for sale prices and errors.

The signature: a pool of light

A soft, warm glow sits behind whatever we want people to notice: the hero desk, the product being viewed, the chat button. It is the brand's only decoration.

  • One glow per screen.
  • Illustrations are flat: oak, slate and lamp gold, no outlines.
  • No photos of people. The rooms are always empty and ready.

Voice

Low and steady, like someone who has their afternoon under control. Answer first, then explain. No exclamation marks, no apologies for routine things.

Say this

"Your desk is still being built, so I can cancel it now. Want me to switch it to the 60-inch instead?"

Not this

"We sincerely apologize for any inconvenience! Let's find your inner calm together and get this sorted!"

Say this

"That order has left our workshop, so our delivery team needs to stop it. I've sent them your details and they'll reply by email today."

Not this

"Unfortunately I'm unable to assist with that request at this time."
