Live with your desk for 30 nights. If it isn't right for your room, we'll pick it up for free and refund you in full.

1. Start a return within 30 nights of delivery, in chat or from your confirmation email.
2. Take the desk apart and pack it. Original boxes are best, but if you no longer have them, we'll bring packing materials.
3. We'll schedule a pickup within 5 business days.
4. Your refund is issued within 5 business days after pickup.

The trial covers desks. Accessories follow our [30-day returns policy]({{ '/returns/' | url }}).
