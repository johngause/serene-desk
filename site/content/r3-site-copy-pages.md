# Serene Desk: page copy

## /shipping/  (title: Shipping and delivery)

**Desks ship free.** Every desk is built to order. Once it leaves our workshop, delivery takes **5 to 7 business days**. Desks arrive in two boxes, one for the frame and one for the tabletop, and the tabletop box needs two people to carry.

**Accessories** ship free on orders over $75, otherwise shipping is a flat $8. Accessories leave within 1 business day and arrive in 3 to 5 business days.

**Tracking.** We email a tracking number the day your order ships. If your order ships in more than one box, you'll get a tracking number for each.

**Current offer:** use code **SERENE10** for 10% off full-price items. Promo codes don't apply to sale items, and you can use one code per order.

## /returns/  (title: Returns)

**Desks** come with our 30-night home trial. See [the trial page](/trial/).

**Accessories** can be returned unused, in their original packaging, within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund.

- You pay return shipping, unless the item arrived damaged or defective. In that case we send a prepaid label.
- Sale items can be returned on the same terms.
- Wool items (the Felt Desk Mat and Acoustic Panels) can't be returned once they've been cut or mounted.
- Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of your return arriving.

To start a return, chat with Wren or reply to your order confirmation email.

## /guides/choosing-a-desk-width/  (title: Choosing a desk width)

**Measure the wall first.** Leave 6 inches of clear wall on each side so the frame can rise without catching trim, outlets or a windowsill.

- **48 inches:** a laptop or one monitor. Fits most bedroom corners.
- **60 inches:** one large monitor or two small ones, with room for a lamp.
- **72 inches:** two large monitors, or a monitor plus space to write or draw.

All Serene Desks are 30 inches deep, except the Ledger Fixed Desk (28 inches) and the Nook Wall Desk (20 inches).

**Height.** The Still Standing Desk adjusts from **25 to 51 inches**, measured to the top of the desk. That suits people from about 5'0" to 6'6", sitting or standing, and it stores 4 heights on the Still Dial.

Ordered the wrong width or finish? If your order is still **Processing**, Wren can switch it for you in chat.

## /about/  (title: About Serene Desk)

Working from home put our desks in the middle of everything: the dishwasher, the dog, the group chat. We build desks that lower the noise instead of adding to it.

Every desk is built to order from solid oak, walnut or ash in our own workshop, finished with a plant-based oil, and shipped in two boxes. The Still Standing Desk rises at 38 dB, quieter than a library, so you can change height on a call without anyone noticing.

We keep the line small on purpose: three desks, a lamp that dims to candlelight warmth, and a handful of quiet accessories.
