| Part | Coverage |
|---|---|
| Desk frames | 10 years |
| Lift motors and the Still Dial | 5 years |
| Tabletops | 2 years |
| Lighting | 2 years |
| Other accessories | 1 year |

Coverage starts on the day of delivery and covers defects in materials and workmanship. It doesn't cover normal wear, water damage or modifications.

To make a claim, chat with Wren and include your order number and a photo. We'll send a replacement part or arrange a repair.
