description: A guide to choosing the right Serene Desk width.
---
**Measure the wall first.** Leave 6 inches of clear wall on each side so the frame can rise without catching trim, outlets or a windowsill.

- **48 inches:** a laptop or one monitor. Fits most bedroom corners.
- **60 inches:** one large monitor or two small ones, with room for a lamp.
- **72 inches:** two large monitors, or a monitor plus space to write or draw.

All Serene Desks are 30 inches deep, except the Ledger Fixed Desk (28 inches) and the Nook Wall Desk (20 inches).

**Height.** The Still Standing Desk adjusts from **25 to 51 inches**, measured to the top of the desk. That suits people from about 5'0" to 6'6", sitting or standing, and it stores 4 heights on the Still Dial.

Ordered the wrong width or finish? If your order is still **Processing**, Wren can switch it for you in chat.
