# Serene Desk: remaining copy

## 1. Desk descriptions (product page intro, above the specs table)

**Still Standing Desk**
A sit-stand desk built to stay out of the way. It rises at 38 dB, quieter than a library, so you can change height mid-call. One wooden dial replaces the usual button panel, with no screen and no beeps, and a built-in cable spine keeps the floor clear. Solid oak, walnut or ash, in 48, 60 or 72 inches.

**Ledger Fixed Desk**
A solid wood desk at a fixed 29 inches, for people who sit and want it to feel like furniture rather than equipment. It has the same wool felt underside and cable tray as our standing desk, in a slimmer 28-inch depth. Oak, walnut or ash, in 48 or 60 inches.

**Nook Wall Desk**
A desk for rooms that are already something else. It mounts to the wall and folds flat to 4 inches when you're done, so the corner goes back to being a corner at 6pm. There's a hidden shelf for a laptop and charger, and a magnetic latch that closes without a click. Oak or ash.
