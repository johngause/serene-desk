**Desks** come with our 30-night home trial. See [the trial page]({{ '/trial/' | url }}).

**Accessories** can be returned unused, in their original packaging, within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund.

- You pay return shipping, unless the item arrived damaged or defective. In that case we send a prepaid label.
- Sale items can be returned on the same terms.
- Wool items (the Felt Desk Mat and Acoustic Panels) can't be returned once they've been cut or mounted.
- Refunds go back to your original payment method within 5 business days of your return arriving.

To start a return, chat with Wren or reply to your order confirmation email.
