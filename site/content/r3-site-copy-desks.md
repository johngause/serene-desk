# Serene Desk: desk features and specs

## Why we exist (one line, home page + About)
Working from home put our desks in the middle of everything: the dishwasher, the dog, the group chat. We build desks that lower the noise instead of adding to it.

## What makes a Serene Desk calm (home page, 3 points)
1. **Quiet lift.** The Still Standing Desk rises at 38 decibels. A library is about 40 dB and a whisper about 30 dB, so it's quieter than the room it's in. You can change height on a call without anyone hearing it.
2. **Nothing dangling.** A built-in cable spine and under-desk tray hold every cord and power strip, so the space under the desk stays as clear as the top.
3. **No beeps, no glare.** One wooden dial controls the height. There's no screen and no beeping. The dial's small light fades out 10 seconds after you let go, so it never glows at you after dark.

## The Still Dial (Still Standing Desk controller)
- Turn the dial to raise or lower. Press it to go to one of **four saved heights**.
- **Soft start and soft stop:** the desk slows for the last inch, so nothing on it jolts or spills.
- **Stand cue (optional, off by default):** the dial glows softly every 50 minutes as a reminder to stand. There's no sound.
- The light fades 10 seconds after use.
